MARBLE DUEL · REKOWSKI

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

This policy explains how Marble Duel, developed by Vladimir Rekowski under the developer name Rekowski, handles information. It describes the current offline and Bluetooth version, which has no advertising, in-app purchases, analytics SDK or developer-operated online service.

You do not need an account to play. Progress and preferences stay on your device. Bluetooth multiplayer exchanges game information directly between the two participating devices.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves unlocked levels, language, sound and music preferences, and match setup choices in local app storage. This information supports progression and remembers your settings. The game does not upload it to the developer or provide cloud synchronisation.

2. Bluetooth multiplayer

Bluetooth play is optional. When you choose it, the app uses Android Bluetooth services to host a match or connect to a device you have paired through Android settings. The device selection dialog reads and displays paired device names and Bluetooth addresses so you can select your opponent. The app does not save a separate history of these identifiers or send them to the developer.

During a match, the participating devices exchange information needed to run and synchronise the game, such as the selected board, turns, rolls, scores, match state and connection messages. This exchange takes place over the Bluetooth connection, without a developer-operated server.

On Android versions that require it, the app requests Nearby devices permissions (Bluetooth Connect and Scan). The Scan permission is used to stop Android discovery before connecting; the app does not perform location tracking. You can decline or revoke these permissions in Android settings and continue playing against the bot or with another person on the same device.

3. Information the game does not request

The current game does not request your name, email address, contacts, camera, microphone, precise location or payment information. It does not include advertising, user accounts, behavioural analytics or in-app purchasing. The current Android build does not request Internet permission.

4. Retention, deletion and control

Local progress and preferences remain on your device until the app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. You can delete them through Android Settings → Apps → Marble Duel → Storage → Clear data (wording may vary). This resets your game progress and preferences.

Bluetooth connection and match information is used while the connection or match is active; the app does not maintain a persistent Bluetooth match history. Pairing records are managed separately by Android and can be removed in Android Bluetooth settings.

5. External links and this policy page

If you choose an external link in the game, it opens in your browser. The destination website handles information under its own policy.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. This page includes no analytics, advertising scripts, tracking pixels or forms added by the developer. For hosting details, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Contacting the developer

For privacy questions, contact Vladimir Rekowski (Rekowski) at wowanium@gmail.com. If you email us, your email provider and ours process the message. We use your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide to respond to and handle your request. Correspondence is kept only for as long as needed for that purpose and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. Please do not send passwords, payment card details or unnecessary personal information.

7. Children’s information

The current game has no registration, chat or personal information entry fields. The developer does not knowingly collect children’s personal information through gameplay. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information directly to the developer may contact the address above to request its deletion.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game’s data practices change, this policy will be updated. Advertising, purchases, analytics or online services will require a revised disclosure before the version containing those features is released, together with any notices or choices required for those features. The effective date above identifies this policy version.
